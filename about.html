About FreeGaming

FreeGaming is a state-of-the-art web gaming portal owned, operated, and maintained by Khan Group of Companies (KGC). Our mission is to make interactive digital entertainment accessible to everyone across the globe — with zero friction, zero paywalls, and zero downloads.

Who We Are

Founded as part of Khan Group of Companies (KGC)'s expanding digital media and technology portfolio, FreeGaming brings together lightweight, high-performance browser games built entirely with modern web standards (HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript).

Whether you are taking a 5-minute break with Cyber Snake, testing your strategic prowess in Neon Tic-Tac-Toe, or challenging your reflexes in Synthwave Highway 84, our portal offers instant entertainment directly in your desktop or mobile browser.

Our Core Values

  • Zero Friction: No mandatory sign-ups, no invasive ads, and no software installations. Click and play immediately.
  • Universal Accessibility: Designed to run fluidly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices of any specification.
  • Digital Innovation: Backed by the vision of Khan Group of Companies to create next-generation web experiences with rich visual aesthetics and responsiveness.
  • Privacy First: We believe gamers should enjoy their favorite pastimes without sacrificing their personal data.
